New Features
Background Variations
You can now create variations of your background videos and images, with adjustments such as blur, brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, greyscale, sepia, invert, opacity and drop shadow. Variations can also zoom, flip, set the background to fit or cover the screen, and change a video’s speed. Variations are shared between locations, but can only be edited or deleted by the location that created them. To change another location’s variation, duplicate it.
Location Control Surface setting
A new setting stops a Control Surface from automatically becoming the Master Control Surface. This is useful when several control surfaces are used for the same location. When the Location Control Surface closes, another control surface now takes over within a few seconds.
